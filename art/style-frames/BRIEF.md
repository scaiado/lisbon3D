# Stage 2a style-frame brief

Purpose: choose the illustration style for Lisbon3D before any proof-scene modelling.
These frames are **concept art only**. They are not game assets and will not ship.

## Fixed for every frame
- **Place:** top of Rua da Bica de Duarte Belo at Calçada do Combro, looking down the funicular street to the Tejo.
- **Camera:** low chase camera, about 2 m high, just behind the microcar; 16:9.
- **Microcar:** original 2-seat bubble car, teal with a cream roof, big round headlights, roof rack with a pastry box.
  It must not copy a real car model.
- **Street:** steep and narrow, with a yellow funicular car halfway down on rails set in stone. Tall narrow
  4–5 storey houses with pastel plaster and azulejo façades, stone window frames, shutters, iron balconies,
  laundry lines, terracotta canal-tile roofs, calçada pavement.
- **Distance:** river, far shore and a suspension-bridge silhouette in warm haze.
- **Light:** late-afternoon gold, coloured shade.
- **Never:** text, logos, real brands, photorealism, generic low-poly.

## Directions
- **A. Gouache miniature:** painted surfaces with soft brush texture and paper grain, gentle tilt-shift, like a diorama.
- **B. Ink-line illustration:** dark-brown linework with watercolour washes, like an architectural travel sketch.
- **C. Painterly storybook realism:** sculpted chunky forms, hand-painted material detail, cinematic light.

## Review checklist for each frame
1. Does it read as Bica / Lisbon?
2. Is the style achievable as real-time 3D in a browser? See the notes for each frame.
3. Is there no copied car design, brand, signage or real artwork?
4. Can it serve as a reference for façade relief, rooflines, ground, light and depth?
